# UK classics in My lists

UK Television 0.9.3 adds two editable lists under **On Demand → My lists**. The one-time migration preserves existing favourites, lists and provider exclusions. You can rename lists, remove titles and delete lists without them reappearing on restart. Titles, original years, descriptions and artwork references are bundled, so the complete lists do not require paging or online searches.

These are curated title records with provider lookup routes, not promises of working episodes. Movy and Dulo use the same title identifiers as normal online search; The Crew uses its existing optional source check on the Firestick. Plex/Tubi/Pluto sources are retained when the normal catalogue supplies matching titles. Provider filters still apply. No external app is added.

Metadata was checked against the public catalogue endpoint already used by the app on 22 October 2026. UK-origin originals were selected; first-air years follow catalogue metadata (sometimes including pilots). Miss Marple uses the Joan Hickson collection and Sherlock Holmes the Jeremy Brett collection. Comedy includes sitcoms, sketch shows and comedy drama; the other list covers crime, drama, period, adventure and science fiction.
